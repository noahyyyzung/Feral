Controls

FlyPoint a palm and squeeze its trigger — you're pushed the opposite way. Palms down to rise, hands behind you to go forward.
RepulsorsPoint a hand forward and pull the trigger.
Charged blastPoint forward, hold grip, release.
MissilesLook at targets to lock on, press A or X.
Wrist rocketsPoint and press B or Y.
Arc LancePalms together in front of your chest + both triggers.
BoostClick either thumbstick.
Fine move / brakeLeft stick (pull back to brake).
TurnRight stick.
MenuLook at the inside of your left wrist. Press buttons with your right finger.
